# Selected Direction — Civic Systems Ledger

## Design Movement
Swiss International Typographic Style blended with contemporary civic-tech reporting.

## Core Principles
1. Evidence before rhetoric: every claim is paired with a source, observation, or limitation.
2. Public-service clarity: dense material is broken into readable blocks, not decorative dashboards.
3. Controlled contrast: warning orange is reserved for verified risk or visible defect; navy and warm white carry the report.
4. Performance-aware presentation: the report itself stays lightweight, responsive, and low-motion.

## Color Philosophy
Warm white is the paper and navy is the institutional ink. A burnt orange signal color marks attention without implying exploitability. Pale blue-gray surfaces separate evidence types while keeping the visual language sober.

## Layout Paradigm
A left rail acts as a persistent evidence index while the main column reads like a long-form audit memo. Findings use asymmetric split panels rather than a centered marketing grid.

## Signature Elements
Numbered evidence markers, thin ledger rules, and small “observed / inferred / not tested” labels.

## Interaction Philosophy
Interactions expose context rather than entertain: tabs switch between observed evidence and interpretation, charts reveal exact values on hover, and the disclosure section clearly separates safe reporting from unauthorized testing.

## Animation
Use only short opacity and transform transitions under 220ms. Respect prefers-reduced-motion. No autoplay, looping hero motion, or scroll-jacking—the report should model the performance discipline it recommends.

## Typography System
Display: Space Grotesk for compact, technical headlines. Body: Source Sans 3 for readable paragraphs. Metadata: IBM Plex Mono for measurements, timestamps, and evidence IDs. Headlines use tight tracking and sentence case; body copy stays between 16–18px with generous line-height.

## Brand Essence
A public-interest teardown of a space-adjacent institution’s web experience, for maintainers and responsible reporters who need evidence rather than spectacle. Personality: exacting, civic, unsentimental.

## Brand Voice
Headlines state the finding plainly. CTAs invite verification, not outrage. Microcopy distinguishes what was observed from what remains untested.

Example lines: “The page is slow before it is useful.” “Report the flaw. Do not weaponize it.”

## Wordmark & Logo
A compact ledger mark: three horizontal navy rules interrupted by one orange orbital arc, suggesting both a report index and a satellite signal. No text inside the mark.

## Signature Brand Color
Signal Orange — #E45B35.
